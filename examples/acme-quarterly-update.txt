Acme Robotics Quarterly Update

Acme Robotics reported that recurring revenue grew 24 percent year over year in
the second quarter. The strongest contribution came from warehouse automation
subscriptions in India and Singapore.

The company opened a small customer-success office in Bengaluru to support its
expansion in India. Management expects the office to shorten onboarding time for
regional customers.

The primary concern for the next quarter is softer demand from small European
manufacturers. Acme plans to keep hiring flat until demand becomes clearer.
